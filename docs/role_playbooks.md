# Practical role playbooks

Roles add responsibilities; they do not remove technical work.

## Scrum Master — `alaamadii`

Primary technical continuity: CI, testing/DevOps, integration readiness, release-candidate coordination, and final defect triage.

Daily practical responsibilities:

- keep board status consistent with real branches and PRs;
- identify dependencies or reviews blocking progress;
- use MP-027 to record only decisions, blockers, owners, and next actions;
- verify reviewer independence;
- help students split work or reduce scope rather than taking all difficult work;
- maintain technical contributions through CI, integration, regression, and release tasks.

The Scrum Master does not write meeting minutes for their own sake and does not become the team’s sole integrator.

## Product Owner — `IslamOuda85`

Primary technical continuity: acceptance map, content baseline, privacy/limitations, calibration protocol, hardening, README, and demo evidence.

Daily practical responsibilities:

- answer scope and acceptance questions using committed documentation;
- keep user-facing wording consistent and non-medical;
- reject unplanned scope that threatens the Sprint Goal;
- verify each completed feature against observable acceptance examples;
- keep documentation and demo scenarios synchronized with actual behavior;
- contribute through documentation-as-code, content configuration, tests/evidence, hardening, and review.

The Product Owner does not rewrite the product vision repeatedly and does not approve incomplete work merely because it is demonstrated once.
