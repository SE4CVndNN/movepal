# System Limitations and Constraints

MovePal uses computer vision and heuristic movement rules to analyze physical exercise form in real-time. While effective for personal tracking and form feedback, the system operates within specific technical and environmental bounds. This document outlines the known limitations of the platform.

---

## 1. Computer Vision and Pose Tracking Limitations

### Camera Positioning and Framing
* **Full-Body Visibility:** Accurate pose estimation requires the user's entire body (or relevant joints) to remain visible within the camera frame throughout the movement.
* **Camera Angle Sensitivity:** Angle calculations in `movement_rules.py` assume a relatively flat, orthogonal side or front profile view depending on the exercise. Extreme high-angle or low-angle camera placement distorts spatial coordinates and results in inaccurate angle measurements.

### Occlusion and Blind Spots
* **Self-Occlusion:** When a body part blocks the view of another (e.g., one arm crossing in front of the torso or overlapping knees during side-profile squats), landmark tracking confidence drops.
* **Loose Clothing:** Oversized or baggy clothing conceals anatomical joints, causing MediaPipe to approximate landmark locations based on fabric contours rather than actual joint centers.

### Lighting and Environment
* **Low Light:** Poor lighting conditions increase noise in video frames, causing landmark jitter and erratic joint angle fluctuations.
* **Background Clutter:** Environments with complex backgrounds or human-shaped objects can cause false landmark detection or decreased model confidence.

---

## 2. Hardware and Performance Constraints

### Processing Power and Frame Rate
* **Real-time Latency:** High-frequency landmark tracking relies on sufficient local or server CPU/GPU resources. Processing frames below 15-20 FPS (frames per second) degrades real-time feedback accuracy and rep counting precision.
* **Camera Resolution:** Low-resolution webcams (below 720p) reduce tracking precision, especially for fine joint adjustments (e.g., wrist position or ankle alignment).

### Single-Person Tracking
* **Multi-Subject Noise:** The current tracking pipeline in `pose_tracking.py` is optimized for a single subject. Multiple people appearing in the camera frame can cause landmark swapping or incorrect pose assignments.

---

## 3. Movement Rule Engine Constraints

### Anatomical Variations
* **Fixed Angle Thresholds:** Static angle thresholds in `movement_rules.py` use generalized biomechanical rules. They do not account for individual mobility restrictions, limb proportion differences (femur-to-torso ratios), or pre-existing injuries.
* **2D Projection Limitations:** Calculating 3D angles from 2D plane projections can introduce minor geometric inaccuracies during complex rotational movements.

---

## 4. Medical and Ergonomic Disclaimer

* **Non-Medical Tool:** MovePal is an automated fitness assistance and form tracking application. It is **not** a clinical diagnostic tool, medical device, or replacement for a certified physical therapist or personal trainer.
* **Injury Prevention:** Automated feedback generated by `feedback.py` is based purely on landmark geometry and does not guarantee complete prevention of physical injury during exercise.
