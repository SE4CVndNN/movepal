# Adult volunteer consent template - draft for course use

This template must be reviewed by the instructor/institution before real collection.

Explain:

- the MovePal prototype and course/research purpose
- what movement samples or recordings are requested
- that participation is voluntary and adults only
- that MovePal is not medical or diagnostic
- where private raw media and derived landmarks are stored
- who can access each form
- how long data is retained
- what may be published or committed
- how to withdraw and request deletion using an anonymous ID
- foreseeable privacy risks and mitigation
- contact for questions

A signed consent record is stored outside the public/project repository. Consent does not authorize unrelated reuse.
