## Purpose

MovePal is a student software-engineering prototype that uses camera images or short samples to extract body-pose landmarks and test simple game rules for movements such as raising both arms, reaching to the side, and lifting a knee or stepping in place.

MovePal is not a medical device, diagnostic tool, clinical rehabilitation system, or replacement for a healthcare professional.

## Why you are invited

You are invited as an adult volunteer to provide a small number of movement samples for software testing, landmark extraction, or prototype threshold calibration.

Participation is voluntary. You may decline any movement or stop at any time without giving a reason.

## What participation involves

If you agree, the team may ask you to:

- stand or sit in front of a camera;
- perform one or more simple movements;
- intentionally perform a correct, incomplete, or borderline example;
- allow the team to record a short image/video sample;
- allow pose landmarks to be derived from the sample.

## Risks and safeguards

Possible risks include:

- visual identification from raw media;
- accidental capture of an identifying background;
- unauthorized access to stored data;
- discomfort while performing a movement.

Safeguards include:

- short, minimal collection;
- neutral backgrounds where possible;
- anonymous IDs;
- private raw storage;
- limited access;
- derived landmarks instead of raw media;
- deletion and withdrawal procedures;
- no child participation.

Do not perform a movement that is uncomfortable. Stop immediately if you feel pain, instability, or concern.
