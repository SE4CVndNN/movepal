# Movement specification template

Each movement specification must define:

1. User-facing name and non-medical purpose.
2. Required landmarks and minimum visibility.
3. Coordinate and anatomical-side convention.
4. Relative-position or joint-angle conditions.
5. Configurable thresholds and the evidence used to choose them.
6. Success, retry, framing, low-confidence, and hold observations.
7. Friendly feedback keys.
8. Positive, negative, borderline, and missing-landmark fixtures.
9. Known limitations and cases intentionally not supported.

## Sprint 1 movements

- Raise both arms
- Side reach, left and right
- Knee lift or step in place, either leg

Rules are heuristic game logic, not exercise, rehabilitation, diagnostic, or medical assessment.
