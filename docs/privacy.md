# Privacy Policy and Data Handling

MovePal is committed to protecting user privacy, particularly when handling real-time video feeds, computer vision data, and personal fitness metrics. This document details how data is collected, processed, stored, and protected within the MovePal platform.

---

## 1. Data We Collect

MovePal processes the following categories of data to provide movement analysis and performance scoring:

* **Video and Camera Stream Data:** Real-time video frames accessed through your camera for live pose estimation.
* **Landmark Coordinates:** Numerical 2D and 3D spatial points (X, Y, Z coordinates) generated by MediaPipe representing joint positions (e.g., shoulders, elbows, knees).
* **Movement Metrics:** Calculated joint angles, repetition counts, movement tempo, and form scores.
* **Session Analytics:** Workout duration, timestamped performance summaries, and historical progress reports.

---

## 2. Video Stream and Computer Vision Processing

We prioritize user privacy by isolating raw visual data from stored telemetry:

* **In-Memory Frame Processing:** Video frames captured by your camera are processed entirely in-memory for real-time landmark detection.
* **No Raw Video Storage:** MovePal does not save, record, or store raw video streams or images to disk unless explicitly enabled by the user for custom feature debugging.
* **Immediate Disposal:** Video frames are immediately discarded from application memory once landmark coordinates are extracted.

---

## 3. How We Use Landmark and Metric Data

Landmark spatial coordinates and calculated metrics are used strictly for system capabilities:

* **Real-time Feedback:** Computing joint angles in `movement_rules.py` to deliver live feedback on form corrections.
* **Scoring Algorithms:** Evaluating posture consistency and rep quality in `scoring.py`.
* **Session Summaries:** Aggregating movement scores into post-workout analytical summaries in `session_summary.py`.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

* **Landmark Fixtures and Logs:** Landmark coordinate arrays stored for testing (`data/landmarks/*.json`) are sanitized, anonymized numerical datasets with all personally identifiable visual metadata removed.
* **Session Records:** Workout summary scores and historical analytics are stored securely and linked to your session or user profile.
* **Data Retention:** Session history is retained only as long as necessary to provide analytics to the user. Users may request full deletion of their session data at any time.

---

## 5. Camera Permissions and User Controls

* **Explicit Permission:** MovePal requests camera access explicitly through the browser or application interface. You can revoke camera permissions at any time via your browser or system settings.
* **Local Processing:** Camera streams are activated only during active exercise tracking sessions and automatically deactivate when a session ends or the tab is closed.

---

## 6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to safeguard your performance data:

* Secure encryption in transit (HTTPS/WSS) for API communications between the client and backend services.
* Strict access controls on backend databases storing user session summaries.
* Complete isolation of computer vision processing pipelines from public-facing endpoints.

---

## 7. Contact Information

For privacy inquiries, data deletion requests, or questions regarding our data handling practices, please open an issue in the project repository or contact the MovePal development team.
