# Sprint retrospective template

## Sprint Goal result

Delivered, partially delivered, or not delivered, with evidence.

## What helped

Technical choices, collaboration, Scrum events, reviews, automation, or learning practices that improved delivery.

## What slowed or blocked us

Dependencies, library compatibility, unclear issues, workload, integration, review delay, or environment problems.

## Evidence

Link issues, PRs, CI, QA findings, and the final release candidate.

## Improvements

Choose a small number of concrete actions with owners for the next project/sprint.

## Technical debt and future backlog

Link existing issues rather than copying vague notes.
